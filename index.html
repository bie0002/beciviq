BeCiviq Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Last Updated: September 18, 2026

BeCiviq (“BeCiviq,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent civic-information platform designed to help people understand proposed legislation, express their views on bills and public issues, compare their positions with elected officials’ votes, and learn about elections, candidates, representatives, and campaign finance.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BeCiviq collects, why we collect it, how it is used and stored, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you. It applies to the BeCiviq mobile application and related services.

Government affiliation notice: BeCiviq is not a government entity and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the United States government or any federal, state, or local government agency.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account and Profile Information

When you create or maintain a BeCiviq account, we may collect:

  • Email address: Used to create and authenticate your account, confirm your identity, support password recovery, communicate with you about your account, and protect the service against unauthorized access.
  • Display name: Used to personalize your profile and identify you within your private account experience.
  • State and ZIP code: Used to personalize civic information, including elections, candidates, representatives, and policy information relevant to your location. Some ZIP codes cross congressional districts, so a ZIP code may not conclusively identify a congressional district.
  • Internal account identifier: Supabase assigns a unique identifier to your account so that your profile and app activity can be associated with the correct authenticated user.

B. Civic-Engagement Activity

When you use BeCiviq, we may collect:

  • Bill reactions: Your selections indicating whether you support, oppose, or are unsure about a bill. These selections are used to provide your personal history, calculate aggregated response totals, and enable comparisons between your positions and recorded votes by elected officials.
  • Saved bills: Records of bills you save or bookmark. This information is used to maintain your saved-bills list and allow you to return to legislation that interests you.
  • Poll responses: Your selections in quick polls, election-candidate polls, and other structured questions. These responses are used to record your participation, prevent unintended duplicate responses where applicable, display results, and calculate aggregated poll totals.

BeCiviq does not treat a user’s reaction or poll response as an official vote, voter-registration record, or communication to a government official.

C. Device, Diagnostic, and Usage Information

BeCiviq and its service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service, such as:

  • Device and operating-system type;
  • App version;
  • Approximate network information, including Internet Protocol address;
  • Dates and times of access;
  • App screens or features accessed;
  • Authentication, security, and server-request logs; and
  • Crash reports, error messages, performance information, and diagnostic events.

We use this information to provide app functionality, diagnose crashes and errors, monitor performance, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve the user experience. BeCiviq does not use this information for third-party targeted advertising.

D. Communications

If you contact BeCiviq for support or provide feedback, we may collect your email address, the contents of your message, screenshots or attachments you choose to provide, and information needed to respond to or resolve your request.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure user accounts;
  • Provide account recovery and customer support;
  • Personalize representatives, elections, candidates, and policy information based on state and ZIP code;
  • Save and display your bill reactions, saved bills, and poll responses;
  • Compare your positions with available voting records of elected officials;
  • Calculate and display aggregated poll or reaction results;
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve BeCiviq;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • Enforce our policies and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of BeCiviq and its users.

3. Supabase and Other Service Providers

BeCiviq uses Supabase as a service provider for account authentication, database hosting, secure server-side functions, and related infrastructure. Supabase processes and stores information on BeCiviq’s behalf so that users can create accounts, sign in, maintain profiles, save bills, submit reactions and poll responses, and delete their accounts.

We may also rely on infrastructure, software, analytics, crash-reporting, email, or distribution providers as necessary to operate the app. These providers may process information only to provide services to BeCiviq, subject to their contractual obligations and applicable law.

BeCiviq does not sell personal information. BeCiviq does not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising or third-party targeted advertising.

4. Public and Government Information

BeCiviq displays information obtained from public and official sources, including:

  • Congress.gov
  • U.S. House of Representatives
  • U.S. Senate
  • Federal Election Commission

This information may include legislation, recorded votes, candidate information, representative information, committee assignments, and campaign-finance records.

Information about public officials, candidates, legislation, and campaign finance is not collected from a user’s private account. Plain-language summaries and the organization or presentation of source information may be created by BeCiviq and should not be understood as official government interpretations.

5. How We Share Information

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers that host, secure, maintain, or support BeCiviq;
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
  • When reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our policies;
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of BeCiviq, our users, or others; or
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of all or part of the service, subject to appropriate privacy protections.

Poll responses and bill reactions may be displayed in aggregated form. BeCiviq does not publicly display the email address associated with an individual response.

6. Retention Periods

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account and profile information: Retained while your BeCiviq account remains active. It is removed from the active system when you complete in-app account deletion.
  • Bill reactions, saved bills, and poll responses: Retained while your account remains active so these features remain available to you and aggregated results can be calculated. User-linked records are deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Authentication and active-session records: Retained while needed to authenticate and secure your account. Active authentication identities and sessions are deleted or invalidated when your account is deleted.
  • Operational, security, diagnostic, and server logs: Generally retained for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary to investigate a security incident, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with law, or resolve a technical issue.
  • Customer-support communications: Generally retained for up to 24 months after the matter is resolved so that we can document the response and address recurring issues.
  • Backups: Residual copies may remain in encrypted or access-controlled backups for up to 30 days after deletion before being overwritten through routine backup rotation. Backup copies are not used for ordinary app functionality and will not be restored except for disaster recovery, security, or legal necessity.

We may retain limited information for longer when required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, or necessary to document and prevent fraud or abuse. When retention is no longer required, the information will be deleted, anonymized, or aggregated so that it is no longer reasonably linked to an identifiable user.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account from within BeCiviq:

  1. Sign in to your BeCiviq account.
  2. Open Your Profile.
  3. Scroll to Account management.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm Delete permanently.

Once confirmed, BeCiviq deletes your authentication account, login identities, active sessions, profile, saved bills, bill reactions, poll responses, and other records directly linked to your account. You will be signed out and will no longer be able to sign in using the deleted account credentials.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Aggregated or de-identified statistics that can no longer reasonably be linked to you may be retained. Polls or other non-personal shared content may also be retained without an association to your deleted profile.

Limited information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, support records, or records that BeCiviq is legally required to maintain, as described in Section 6.

If you cannot access the in-app deletion feature because you cannot access your account, contact mcbrian2012@gmail.com for assistance.

8. Data Security

BeCiviq uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include authenticated access, access controls, encrypted network transmission, and restricted administrative functions.

No electronic system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may:

  • Review and update available profile information in the app;
  • Choose whether to react to bills, save bills, or participate in polls;
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests; and
  • Permanently delete your account using the in-app deletion process.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

To submit a request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

10. Children’s Privacy

BeCiviq is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate. Privacy and data-protection laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BeCiviq changes or as legal and operational requirements evolve. When we make changes, we will revise the “Last Updated” date above.

If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice when reasonably appropriate.
